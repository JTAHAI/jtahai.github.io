The Procedural Grand Prix source project presented the model as a lowered black Mk IV with owner-specific fitment details. DISSENT uses that optimized geometry as the central vehicle body and adds original fictional flight hardware, including six-axis wings, thrusters, cannons, roll fins, emissive accents, and game-specific color treatments.

The Volkswagen name and vehicle shape are used descriptively. This game is not sponsored by or affiliated with Volkswagen.
